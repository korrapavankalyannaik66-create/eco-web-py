Who We Are

About EcoLoop Platform

🏛️ Govt Regulated Oversight

Every transaction is monitored and verified by registered Government Officers to ensure zero toxic waste dumping.

🏭 Certified Recyclers

We connect sellers directly with authorised recycling companies, guaranteeing proper extraction of precious metals.

💰 Fair Market Value

Real-time base pricing for laptops, mobile phones, PCB circuit boards, and copper wiring with transparent payouts.

What We Offer

Our Recycling Services

📦 Doorstep Pickup

Schedule e-waste pickups directly from your location with real-time GPS route tracking.

🛡️️ Data Destruction

Secure hardware destruction and disk wipe certifications before recycling processing.

💳 Instant Disbursal

Direct payouts via UPI or Bank Transfer immediately after physical material inspection.

How EcoLoop Works
